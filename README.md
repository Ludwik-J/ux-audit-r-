# UX Audit of an Online Pet Shop (2026)

A usability audit of three websites of an online shop selling dog beds and
other pet accessories, conducted in 2026 during a UX internship at Royalpet.

## Objective
To identify usability barriers that could hinder customers from browsing and
purchasing products, and to propose practical, prioritised improvements.

## Scope
- 3 websites belonging to the company
- Evaluation of navigation, language versions and overall consistency of the
  user experience

## Method
- **Heuristic evaluation** of each website against established usability
  principles
- **Severity rating** of every issue found, from most to least critical
- **Prioritised recommendations** describing what to fix first and why

## Examples of top-severity findings
- The English-language version had no functioning menu, leaving
  international visitors unable to navigate the site.
- The website URL had a completely different name from the one displayed,
  which undermines brand consistency and user trust.
